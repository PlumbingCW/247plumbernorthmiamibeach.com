Sewer backup emergency: what to do first

When sewage backs up into a tub, shower or floor drain, treat it as an emergency. Stop running water, do not flush, keep children and pets away from the wet area and call our 24/7 line. Sewer line repair in North Miami Beach is booked same-day when the schedule allows, and the first job is to find out whether the problem is a clog or a broken pipe.

A clog can be cleared. A cracked, offset or collapsed pipe has to be repaired, and that is a different tool, a different price and sometimes a permit question. We use a camera to see which one you have before talking about repair.

How we decide between clearing and repairing

The camera tells the story. A clean pipe with a solid clog needs clearing. A pipe with roots through a joint, a sag that holds water, or a crack with soil visible behind it needs repair.

Callers often describe it as a localized crack with roots coming in. That is a classic spot repair candidate, and we say so when the video shows it. If the damage runs long, we explain why a spot repair would not last.

Roots, old materials and slab homes

In a slab home the sewer line runs under the floor, so repairs near the house can mean cutting concrete. We locate the damage with a camera and a locator before anyone breaks ground, so the opening is as small as the job allows.

Older drain lines in a city this age can be cast iron, clay or early plastic, and each fails in its own way. Cast iron scales and cracks, clay joints let roots in, and early plastic can sag. For a full pipe check, see sewer camera inspection.

Septic or public sewer?

Most callers are on the public sewer, but some properties in the area may still use a septic tank, and the diagnosis is different. Tell us which one you have, or say you are not sure, and we will help you find out. Do not assume the street connection is the problem until the line has been checked.

Before any digging starts

Florida law requires notice before most digging. Sunshine 811 says to notify it at least two full business days before you dig, and you can reach it by calling 811. We handle the locate request for work we perform.

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing is the statewide plumbing code. Its 2023 edition is built on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Florida amendments. Permit rules depend on the job and the municipality, so ask the building department that covers your address what applies.

After the repair

We run water through the line, confirm flow at every fixture and give you the video record when a camera was used. Permit rules for sewer work vary, so ask the City of North Miami Beach building department about the job before digging.

If the line is clear but the house still smells, a leak in a drain pipe may be the cause. Leak detection tests that.
