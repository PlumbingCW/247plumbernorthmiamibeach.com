Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

Call (305) 850-6382

North Miami Beach 24/7 Plumbing ("we", "us") takes the privacy of callers and website visitors seriously. This page explains what we collect, why and what choices you have.

Calls and call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call and we will discuss other ways to help you, such as email.

Information we collect

When you call or email, we may collect your name, phone number, address, a description of the plumbing problem and details needed to schedule a visit. We use this information to respond, to schedule and perform work, to follow up on a visit and to keep records.

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask for information on the site itself.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

Third party services

  • Maps on this site are provided by Google Maps. Loading a map may send information such as your IP address to Google, under Google's own privacy terms.
  • Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which may also receive your IP address.
  • Our site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the people who need it to perform your service, with service providers who help us run our business and when the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep service records and call recordings for as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and insurance obligations, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal limits. Email contact@247plumbernorthmiamibeach.com or call (305) 850-6382.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
