Plumbing installation for remodels and new builds

Plumbing installation in North Miami Beach covers new fixtures, relocated drains, added bathrooms, kitchen remodels and new construction rough-in. We plan the work with you and with the other trades so pipes land where the finished room needs them.

This is planned work. If something is leaking right now, see burst pipe repair for emergencies.

What we install

  • Sinks, toilets, tubs, showers and laundry hookups.
  • Water heaters, including planned water heater installation.
  • Shutoff valves, supply lines and hose bibs.
  • New drain and vent lines for additions.
  • Rough-in plumbing for new construction.

Remodels in older homes

In a house that has been standing for decades, opening a wall can reveal galvanized or cast iron pipe that does not match the new work. We tell you what we find and what it means for the plan, and repiping may be worth pricing at the same time.

Permit rules vary, so ask the City of North Miami Beach building department about the work you plan.

Bathroom additions and moving drains

Adding a bathroom or moving a toilet means new drain and vent lines, and the route depends on the floor. On a slab, the drain has to be cut into the concrete, and the vent must reach the roof or tie into an existing vent.

We lay out the route before anyone cuts, explain what has to open and give you a scope that includes patching or leaves it to your flooring contractor, whichever you prefer.

Kitchen remodels

A kitchen remodel often adds a new sink position, a pot filler, a dishwasher and an ice maker line. Each one needs a supply, a drain or both, and a shutoff you can reach.

We install the rough-in before cabinets and the finish plumbing after. For appliance connections, see garbage disposal installation.

A simple order of operations

  1. Walk the space

    We look at the existing pipes, access and where fixtures will go.

  2. Quote and plan

    You get a scope, a schedule and a clear price.

  3. Rough-in

    Supply, drain and vent lines go in before walls close.

  4. Inspection

    Permit rules vary, so we confirm with the building department what applies.

  5. Trim and test

    Fixtures are set, valves tested and leaks checked.

Laundry rooms, outdoor hookups and utility spaces

A laundry room needs a standpipe or a box drain, hot and cold supplies and a shutoff you can reach without moving the machine. We also install hose bibs, outdoor showers and utility sinks, and we set drains at the right height so the water leaves cleanly.

Outdoor lines should drain or shut off from inside the house so a broken hose bib does not become a slow leak. We label the valves before we leave.

Working with your contractor and your inspector

Plumbing has to land before walls close and before the floor is poured or finished. If you have a general contractor, we coordinate dates with them and with the electrician and framer, so no one waits on a pipe.

We also tell you what to expect at inspection. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of North Miami Beach building department which permits and inspections apply to your project.

Permits and inspections on new work

New plumbing work is covered by the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the IPC. Many projects need a permit and an inspection before walls are closed. Permit rules depend on the job and the municipality, so ask the building department that covers your address what applies. You can verify any contractor's license on the state's Verify a Licensee page at myfloridalicense.com.

New construction

For new builds, we do rough-in and trim. We coordinate with the contractor, set the schedule and inspect before covering. Tell us the plan set and the timeline when you call.
