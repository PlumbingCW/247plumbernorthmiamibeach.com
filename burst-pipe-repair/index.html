Burst pipe emergency: stop the water first

A burst pipe sends water through a room in minutes, so the first move is the main shutoff valve. Turn it clockwise or perpendicular to the pipe, open the lowest faucets to drain the lines and switch off power to any wet area you cannot reach safely. Then call our 24/7 line. Burst pipe repair in North Miami Beach is booked same-day when the schedule allows.

Florida's warm climate means freezing is rarely the cause here. More often it is corrosion, a failed fitting, pressure, a nail or screw through a line or an old pipe that finally gave up.

Before we arrive

  1. Shut off the main valve

    It is usually near where the water line enters the house or at the meter box. Know where it is before you need it.

  2. Cut power to wet areas

    Do not stand in water near outlets or appliances. Use the breaker if it is safe.

  3. Drain the lines

    Open a low faucet to relieve pressure.

  4. Contain and photograph

    Move valuables, use towels or a wet vac, and take photos for insurance.

Copper, pinholes and small leaks

Many repair calls are not a spectacular burst. They are a pinhole in a copper line, a sweating joint or a split near a fitting. These leak slowly and damage cabinets, drywall and flooring over weeks.

A repair is often a short section replaced. If the pipe is thin and corroded in several places, patching will keep failing, and repiping becomes the better answer. For leaks you cannot find, see leak detection.

Condos and multi-unit buildings

A burst pipe in a condo can flood the unit below, so speed matters and so does who you notify. Tell your association or building manager, shut off the unit valve and call us.

We document where the pipe failed and whether it sits on your side of the shutoff, which helps sort out responsibility and insurance between owners and the association.

Insurance and photos

Take photos before cleanup, keep damaged items and save receipts. Many policies cover sudden water damage, and your insurer will ask what failed and when. We give you a written description of the repair.

If the burst was on the buried line between the meter and the house, ask your insurer how that is treated.

Pipe materials and what the code says

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the IPC, lists which pipe materials may be used for water supply. When we replace a failed run, we use an approved material and test the line under pressure before closing the wall.

After the repair

We test the new section under pressure and check the area for other leaks. We also show you where the main valve is and test it, because a stuck shutoff valve is a problem you want to learn about before the next emergency.

If the burst sat on the water line between the meter and the house, our water service line repair page covers that part.
