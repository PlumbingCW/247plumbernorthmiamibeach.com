A toilet that overflows is an emergency

A toilet that overflows or leaks at the base needs attention right away. Our toilet repair plumbers in North Miami Beach answer the line 24/7 and offer same-day appointments when available. Turn the supply valve behind the toilet clockwise, lift the tank lid and push the flapper closed if water is rising, and keep the floor dry until we arrive.

Most toilet calls fall into four groups: clogs, tank parts that fail, leaks at the base and installs. We sort which one you have on the phone, so the plumber arrives with the right parts.

What to do if it is overflowing

  1. Stop the water

    Turn the supply valve behind or beside the toilet clockwise until it stops.

  2. Lift the flapper

    If the tank is feeding the bowl, press the flapper down to stop the flow.

  3. Do not flush again

    Another flush sends more water into the bowl.

  4. Protect the floor

    Use towels and a bucket, and keep power cords away from the water.

Clogs and what they mean

A single clogged toilet is usually a local blockage and a plunger or auger clears it. A toilet that clogs with other drains acting up points to the main line, and that is a different job. See drain cleaning for how we sort that out.

Repeat clogs on a toilet may mean a partial blockage deeper in the line or a toilet that is not flushing as designed. We test both.

A toilet that runs often wastes water and can raise the bill. If your meter keeps moving with the house quiet, see leak detection.

Wax rings, flanges and leaks at the base

Water at the base of the toilet usually means the wax ring has failed. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange and the subfloor, replace the ring and reset the toilet level.

A damaged flange or soft subfloor changes the job, and we tell you before proceeding. Persistent smell around the base is another sign the seal is failing.

Choosing a replacement toilet

Toilets differ in bowl shape, flush design and height. A taller comfort-height bowl helps some people, while a compact elongated bowl fits tighter spaces. We stand in the room with you and check the rough-in distance, which is the measure from the wall to the flange.

Buying the wrong size is the most common install mistake, and a quick measurement prevents it.

When to call right away

  • Water is spreading across the floor or toward electrical items.
  • Other drains are gurgling or backing up with the toilet.
  • Sewage smell or a stain at the base of the toilet that is growing.
  • The toilet rocks and leaks when used.

Fixture standards worth knowing

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the IPC, sets maximum flush volumes for new toilets in the state. A new fixture should carry the maker's label showing its rated volume. Ask us which models suit your drain and flush needs.

Installing a new toilet

A new toilet is more than a swap. We check the flange height, the supply valve and the shutoff, and set the toilet level with a new ring and bolts.

For a bathroom remodel, we coordinate with the vanity and the shower, and our plumbing installation page lists the rest of the work we do.
