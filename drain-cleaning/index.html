When a clogged drain cannot wait

A drain that stops moving water becomes an emergency the moment it threatens a floor, a ceiling below or the only bathroom in the house. Our drain cleaning plumbers answer the line 24/7 in North Miami Beach and book same-day appointments when the schedule allows. Until a plumber arrives, stop running water, leave the toilet unflushed and lift rugs and boxes away from any drain that is gurgling or rising.

Most clogs come from grease, hair, wipes or soap scum. A few come from something harder to see, such as roots pushing into an older line or a section of pipe that has sagged. Which one you have decides whether a cable clears it in an hour or the line needs a camera and a bigger tool, and we sort that out on the phone first.

Signs the clog is in the main line, not just the sink

One slow sink usually has a local clog. Several symptoms at once point to the shared line that leaves the house, and that changes the tool and the price.

  • Two or more fixtures are slow at the same time, such as a tub and a toilet.
  • Water backs up into a shower or tub when the washing machine drains.
  • Floor drains gurgle or bubble when someone flushes.
  • A plunger or store-bought cleaner helps for a few weeks and the clog returns.
  • A sewage smell hangs around a cleanout cap or a floor drain.

If you see two or three of these, say so when you call. It tells the plumber to bring a drum machine instead of a hand auger.

Kitchen sinks, disposals and grease

Kitchen lines carry the heaviest load in a house. Cooking fat cools inside the pipe, coats the walls and catches food solids until the opening shrinks to a trickle.

A double sink with a disposal adds a trap, a tailpiece and a dishwasher hose that all feed the same drain. If the disposal hums but the sink stays full, the clog may sit past the disposal, and a jammed unit needs separate work, which we cover under garbage disposal repair. Never pour hot grease down the drain, because it only moves the problem farther down the line.

How a drain cleaning visit works

  1. Call and describe the drain

    Tell us which fixtures are affected, whether water is on the floor and whether this has happened before.

  2. Quick checks first

    We ask about the washing machine, the nearest cleanout and any recent work on the pipes.

  3. Cable the line

    The plumber runs a cable with the right cutting head through the fixture trap or the cleanout and clears the blockage.

  4. Confirm it flows

    Water runs through every affected fixture before the plumber calls the job done.

  5. Camera if it keeps returning

    A repeat clog gets a camera look so the cause is found instead of cleared again. See sewer camera inspection.

Which tool fits which clog

The tool matters as much as the effort. Using a hand auger on a main line wastes an hour, and jetting a sink trap is overkill.

Drain tools and what each one is for
ToolBest forLimit
Hand augerOne sink, tub or shower trapCannot reach the main line.
Drum machine cableBranch lines and main lines, light rootsLeaves some grease film on the pipe wall.
Hydro jetterHeavy grease, scale and clogs that keep returningNot for a cracked or collapsed pipe.
Inspection cameraUnknown cause or repeat clogsFinds the problem but does not clear it.

When a line needs more than a cable, we step up to hydro jetting rather than cabling the same clog twice.

Why your drain needs a cleanout

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing is the statewide plumbing code. Its 2023 edition is built on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Florida amendments. Plumbing systems are built so drains can be reached for cleaning, which is why a cleanout cap sits in the yard or on an outside wall. Show us where yours is when we arrive.

What a century of building means for North Miami Beach pipes

NMB Water marks the city's centennial in its 2025 water quality report, and a city that old has been built out in layers. A single street can mix cast iron, clay and PVC drain lines, and the old materials roughen over time so grease and roots catch more easily.

That matters on a service call. We look at the age and material of the pipe before choosing a cutting head, and if a drain line is leaking into the soil or slab we point you to leak detection rather than clearing it again and again.
