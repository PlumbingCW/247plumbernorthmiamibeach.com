When the leak starts in someone else's unit

Aventura is dense with condominiums, and the most common plumbing emergency is not a burst pipe in your own wall. It is water coming through a ceiling from a unit above or backing up from a shared drain stack. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and we start by finding out whether the problem is inside your unit or in the building.

If it is in the building, the association has to act, and we write down what we see to help you reach them. If it is yours, we use leak detection to trace the source before any wall is opened.

Before we arrive: leaks and backups in an Aventura condo

In a condo the first minutes decide whether the neighbors get wet too. Work through these before the plumber reaches you.

  • Close the shutoff valve for your unit. It may be in a hall closet, under a sink or at the water heater.
  • Call building management or the front desk and ask them to close the riser if water is still flowing.
  • Unplug electronics in the wet area and move valuables from the floor.
  • Call the 24/7 line and give us the building, the unit and the floor.

If you cannot reach the unit shutoff, tell management immediately. A riser valve can stop the flow for the whole stack.

Aventura in brief

Aventura incorporated as a city in 1995 and is compact, with the Intracoastal Waterway on its east side. The Aventura Mall, opened in 1983, anchors the commercial district, and the William Lehman Causeway is the main east-west route. Shops and offices cluster around both, which is why after-hours plumbing help matters to tenants here.
