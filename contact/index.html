What to tell us when you call

  • The street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see: where the water is, how fast and what color.
  • What you have already shut off.
  • If you are in a condo, the building and unit.
  • A phone number to reach you.

With that, we can say what to do next and what kind of visit is needed.

Hours and response

The phone line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. Same-day appointments are offered when available. We do not promise exact arrival times, because traffic, weather and open calls change them, but we will tell you what to expect when you call.
