Home base: North Miami Beach

Our office is at 3500 NE 163rd St in North Miami Beach, ZIP 33160. The city has neighborhoods such as Eastern Shores, Highland Village, Fulford, Uleta, Sunray and Oakgrove, with US-1 (Biscayne Boulevard) on the east side and I-95 along the northwest.

The city operates its own water system. According to NMB Water's 2025 Consumer Confidence Report, water comes from the Biscayne and Floridan aquifers and is treated at the city-owned Norwood Water Treatment Plant before delivery. The report says the city found no lead service lines in its system, and also notes that lead can come from household plumbing and fixtures.

If your water looks brown or yellow, the report says it can point to rust or aging pipe, and it recommends having a licensed plumber take a look. Our water heater repair and repiping pages explain what that usually involves.
