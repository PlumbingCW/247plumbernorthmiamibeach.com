The historic core of North Miami Beach

Miami-Dade County historic preservation records say developer Lafe Allen bought the lands of Captain William Fulford in 1923 to 1925 and created Fulford-by-the-Sea. The City of Fulford was incorporated in 1927 and rechartered as North Miami Beach in 1931. The Fulford-By-The-Sea Monument on Fulford Boulevard near US 1 is now a county historic site, and in 1936 the old power plant facing Victory Park became City Hall.

A local planning summary lists Fulford as part of the City Center district, alongside Government Center and University Park, with historic homes and commercial uses. A neighborhood data site puts many residences in Fulford Highlands, ZIP 33162, as built between 1940 and 1969, with a mix of small single-family homes, apartment complexes and high-rises.

A mix of homes, apartments and owners and renters

Older streets with a mix of housing mean a wide range of plumbing ages in one area. A 1950s house can have its original drain line, while the house next door may have been repiped. We look at what is actually in the wall before we say anything.

For apartments and rentals, we coordinate with the owner or manager. A drain cleaning call in a building may involve a shared stack, and that needs the manager to be informed. If the clog keeps returning, a sewer camera inspection shows where it is.

Before we arrive: if you have a leak in Fulford

Permits and a note about older buildings

Repairs and replacements in North Miami Beach follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Some jobs need a permit, and we say which ones when we quote.

Your water bill shows your utility. NMB Water publishes its yearly Consumer Confidence Report, which says brown or yellow water can point to rust or aging pipe and recommends a licensed plumber take a look.
