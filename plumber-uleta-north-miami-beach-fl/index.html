Where Uleta sits and what it is like

A local planning summary places Uleta west of NE 6th Avenue and north of SR 826, bounded by I-95 on the northwest. It describes the area as mostly single-family homes, with some duplexes, triplexes and commercial edges. Real estate listings describe many ranch-style homes from the 1950s, with fenced yards, palms and carports, and show most listings on NE 169th through NE 176th Street.

Uleta Park has a pool, ball fields and a community center. The Uleta Community Center was completed in 1966, according to the city history on Wikipedia. The Golden Glades Interchange and the Golden Glades Tri-Rail station are close, so many residents commute from here.

Owners and landlords have different plumbing questions

Because Uleta has duplexes and some rentals, a good number of calls come from landlords and tenants. If you rent, tell your landlord first. If you own, ask the tenant for access times and the unit number when you call.

For a rental, a clear price before work starts matters. We explain what we found and what it costs, and you decide. A water heater repair often costs less than replacement, but an old tank that has leaked before is a different case.

What we help with in Uleta

Single-family and small multi-family properties tend to call for these services.

  • Backed-up drains and repeat clogs, starting with drain cleaning and a camera when needed.
  • Slab and under-house leaks, covered on the slab leak repair page.
  • Toilet and faucet repairs that tenants report, on the toilet repair page.
  • Small shops and offices on the commercial edges, under commercial plumbing.

Ranch-style homes built on slabs hide most of their pipes. A hot spot on the floor or a bill that suddenly rises are common signs, though a meter test is the way to be sure.

Before we arrive: if you have a leak in your Uleta home or rental

These steps keep a small leak from becoming a larger one.

  • Close the fixture shutoff, or the main valve at the meter.
  • Switch off power at the breaker for the wet area.
  • If you are a tenant, tell the landlord or manager now.
  • Call us with the street, the unit and what the water is doing.

If the main valve is stuck, tell us on the call.

When it looks like a leak but may not be

Turn off every fixture and look at the water meter. If the leak indicator or dial moves, water is escaping somewhere. If it does not move, the sound or the damp spot has another cause.

Water quality questions belong with your utility. Your bill shows which one serves you, and NMB Water publishes its yearly Consumer Confidence Report for customers.

Repairs and replacements here follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and we say which jobs need a permit when we quote.
