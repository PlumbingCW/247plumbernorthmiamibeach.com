What we do

North Miami Beach 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 3500 NE 163rd St in North Miami Beach. We take emergency calls around the clock and book planned work in the daytime. The work covers drains, sewer lines, leaks, water heaters, fixtures and repiping for homes and small businesses.

We serve North Miami Beach and nearby towns including North Miami, Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach, Miami Gardens and Miami Shores. You can read about each on its own page under service areas.

What licensed and insured means

Florida requires many plumbing contractors to hold a state license, and you can check any license yourself. The Department of Business and Professional Regulation runs a Verify a Licensee page at myfloridalicense.com that lets you search by name or license number.

Plumbing work in Florida must meet the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). We are licensed and insured. Use the same page to check any contractor who comes to your door, including us, and ask for proof of insurance if you want to see it.

What we will not do

  • Quote a price we cannot explain.
  • Promise an exact arrival time we cannot control.
  • Recommend a replacement when a repair will do.
  • Open walls or floors on a guess.

If a problem is outside what we do, such as a gas leak, we say so and tell you who to call. For a gas smell, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911.

What a first call sounds like

The person who answers will ask for your address, a phone number and a short description of what is happening. They will also ask whether water is still running, whether anyone is in danger and whether you have found the shutoff.

From there we tell you what to do in the next few minutes and what kind of visit fits. If the problem is serious, we send a plumber as soon as one is available. If it can wait, we offer a same-day appointment when available or a time that suits you. You never need to know the plumbing term for what you see. Describe it as you would to a neighbor.

Who we help

  • Homeowners in single-family houses, many of them built on slabs.
  • Condo and townhome owners who share stacks, risers and rules with neighbors.
  • Landlords and property managers who need a clear record of each visit.
  • Restaurants, shops and offices that cannot afford to lose a service day.

Each group has different constraints, and we plan the visit around them. For example, a condo job starts with the building rules, and a restaurant job starts with the hours when the kitchen is closed.

How to choose any plumber

Whether you hire us or someone else, a few checks protect you. Look up the license on the state site. Ask for proof of insurance. Ask what the price covers and what could change it. Ask who will actually come to the door.

Be careful of anyone who quotes a large price over the phone without seeing the problem, who asks for full payment before work starts or who pushes a replacement without showing you why. A good plumber shows you the failed part and explains it in plain words.

A local view of the work

North Miami Beach water comes from the city utility, NMB Water, which draws from the Biscayne and Floridan aquifers and treats the water at the Norwood plant. Neighboring North Miami runs its own water utility. Knowing which utility serves a house matters when you ask about pressure, taste or hardness.

Many homes in the area sit on slabs, and many condominiums share stacks and risers. That shapes how we find a leak, how we cut an opening and who needs to be told. Our location pages go into the details.
