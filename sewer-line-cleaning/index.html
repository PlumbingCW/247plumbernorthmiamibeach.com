Whole house backing up? This is an emergency

The main sewer line is the pipe that takes every drain in the house out to the street connection. When it clogs, the lowest fixtures back up first, usually a floor drain, a shower or a tub on the ground floor. Our 24/7 line in North Miami Beach takes these calls any hour, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

Until a plumber arrives, stop running water, skip the washing machine and dishwasher and avoid flushing. Every gallon you send down goes to the lowest opening in the house, and that is where the damage starts.

How we clear a main line

We start at the closest cleanout, which is a capped opening on the pipe, often in the yard or near the house wall. A drum machine with a cutting head opens the blockage, and we follow with water to confirm the flow.

If the clog returns or the cable will not pass, we move to hydro jetting or a camera. A line that clogs twice in a season usually has a cause, such as roots or a sag, and that needs to be found.

Sewer backup: what to do first

  • Stop using water throughout the house.
  • Keep people and pets away from standing water, which may contain sewage.
  • Turn off power to any wet area you cannot safely reach.
  • Move belongings off the floor if you can do so safely.
  • Take photos of the water and any damage before cleanup.

The photos matter if you later file an insurance claim.

Where the line ends and the city begins

A private lateral usually runs from the house to the connection with the public sewer. Who owns which part depends on where the problem sits, so ask your sewer utility before you assume a cost. Plumbing inside the property follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the IPC.

Homes, condos and small buildings

The sewer line for a single-family house is a different job from a condo stack or a building lateral. In a condo, a backup can come from the shared stack, which sits above your unit and is not yours to clear, so tell the association and call us. We cover the building side on our commercial plumbing page.

Some older neighborhoods have a mix of materials in the same line. A camera before and after cleaning shows whether the pipe is sound, and sewer line repair is the next step if it is not.

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

Severity of the blockage
A soft clog is quick. Roots or a packed line take longer.
Cable or jetter
Jetting costs more than cabling because it does more.
Time of day
After-hours emergencies are priced above scheduled daytime work.
